About the gallery

One of Armenia's leading galleries

Founded in 2012 by art expert Armine Antikyan, the gallery is a home for modern Armenian art and for the artists who make it.

Since it opened, Antikyan Gallery has organised more than twenty group and solo exhibitions in Armenia and abroad. Alongside traditional museum and gallery spaces, it regularly looks for alternative locations for contemporary art events and exhibitions.

The gallery represents artists working in painting, drawing, sculpture and installation. It also represents a number of foreign artists in Armenia, among them the Italian artist Bruno Bruni.
